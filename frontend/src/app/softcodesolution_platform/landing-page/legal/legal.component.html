Data Safety Protocols

Enterprise-grade security for your business data

SoftCode Solution SMC-Private Limited · Zero-Knowledge Architecture
enhanced_encryption

All client data is protected under PECA 2016 and Companies Act 2017. SoftCode Solution SMC-Private Limited maintains strict zero-knowledge architecture — your business records remain exclusively yours.

Section 3.1

Encryption Standards

All data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 standards. Data in transit is protected via TLS 1.3. These measures comply with cybersecurity standards recognized under PECA 2016 for electronic data protection in Pakistan.

Section 3.2

Confidentiality & NDA

SoftCode Solution SMC-Private Limited enters into a binding confidentiality agreement with every client, enforceable under the Contract Act 1872 (Pakistan) and Companies Act 2017. We pledge never to disclose, share, or use your business intelligence, sales records, customer lists, or financial data for any purpose other than providing the agreed-upon service.

Section 3.3

Disaster Recovery

Our infrastructure includes geographically redundant backups updated every 5 minutes. In the event of a catastrophic server failure, our Disaster Recovery protocol initiates automatic failover to a secondary node within 60 seconds, minimizing data loss to near-zero (RPO < 5 minutes).

Section 3.4

Intrusion Detection & Response

We employ AI-driven behavioral analysis to detect unauthorized access patterns in real time. Any suspicious activity triggers an immediate lockdown of the affected tenant environment. Any confirmed breach will be reported as required under PECA 2016, and you will be informed within 2 hours of detection.

Section 3.5

Access Control

Role-based access control (RBAC) ensures team members can only access data relevant to their assigned roles. All login events, data exports, and administrative actions are logged in an immutable audit trail accessible to the business owner at any time, in compliance with Companies Act 2017 record-keeping requirements.
